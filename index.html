About

Research with practical impact

A profile built around intelligent systems, visual understanding, and data-driven healthcare and industry applications.

Dr. Jamal Hussain Shah is a Tenured Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at COMSATS University Islamabad, Wah Campus, Pakistan. He completed his PhD in Control Science and Engineering in the Department of Automation at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), Hefei, China.

His academic work combines teaching, research, student supervision, and collaboration on applied AI systems. His research has addressed visual recognition, medical image analysis, intelligent monitoring, malware classification, and agricultural and environmental applications.

He has served in academic leadership and departmental roles, including AI domain leadership, graduate coordination, internship and industrial liaison responsibilities, and academic review committees.
